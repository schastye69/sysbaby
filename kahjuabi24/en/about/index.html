About us

We are a team of specialists in insurance, vehicle inspection and vehicle valuation. We provide professional advice and full support with registering and settling insurance claims after a road accident.

We take care of the entire paperwork: we help you fill in the claim form, prepare the necessary documents, photograph the damage to your car and send the documents to the insurance company ourselves.

Our work doesn't stop at registering the claim. We have our own car service, where we carry out quality repairs and restore cars after accidents.

Thanks to many years of experience, professional knowledge and well-established cooperation with insurance companies, we help our clients save time, protect their interests and avoid unnecessary hassle.

Our services

We provide full support at every stage — from registering the insurance claim to repairing and restoring your car — all the services you need in one place!

  • Accident and CASCO insurance claims

    We fill in the claim form, prepare the documents and register the case with the insurance company.

  • Glass damage claims

    Cracks and chips in the windscreen or side windows — we register the claim and arrange replacement or repair.

  • Claims abroad — registration and follow-up

    An accident abroad or involving a foreign party — we help with the documents and with communicating with insurers.

  • Damage assessment and vehicle inspection

    Inspection, photo documentation of the damage and a repair estimate for the insurance company.

  • Advice and support on insurance matters

    We explain your rights and stay with you until the payout or the end of the repair.

  • Repair and restoration in our own car service
