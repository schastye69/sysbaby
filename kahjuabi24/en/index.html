Frequently asked questions

Draft — please review

What should I do right after an accident?

Record what happened: photograph the scene, the position of the cars relative to each other with the registration plates visible, and any visible damage. Write down the other car's registration number and the other driver's name and contact details — you can use the sample statement on our website.

Do I have to fill in the documents myself?

No. If the circumstances allow, come to the Kahjuabi24 office: we will help you fill in the claim form, prepare the necessary documents, photograph the damage and send the documents to the insurance company.

What information will I need?

Photos of the scene and the damage, the other car's registration number, and the other driver's name and contact details. We will help you prepare the rest.

How much does a consultation cost?

It depends on your situation. Call us or leave a request on the website — we will explain the terms before any work starts.

Can I get a car while mine is being repaired?

Yes. We provide a replacement car for the duration of the repair, as well as rental cars.

Which insurers do you work with?

We handle motor and comprehensive (KASKO) claims, glass damage and claims abroad, and deal with the insurance companies directly — you will find them listed under “Insurance companies”.

What if my car can't be driven?

Call our partner — tow truck 24h: +372 5844 2244. They arrange towing and transport, and also help with jump-starts and unlocking the car.

Do you repair cars yourselves?

Yes. We have our own car service where we repair and restore cars after accidents and check the quality of the repair.
