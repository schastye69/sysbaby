Replacement and rental cars

You should not have to walk. With our help you are always on wheels!

01

Replacement car

If the repair of your vehicle takes five working days or more and you need a car during that time, we will provide one.

Tell us you need a replacement car while we are scheduling the repair. That way you can be sure a car will be waiting when you bring yours in. The replacement car is used while your own vehicle is being repaired and is returned when you collect it.

The replacement car user undertakes to:

  • Return the car with the same amount of fuel as when it was collected.
  • Pay any fines incurred during the rental period — parking fines and speeding fines, for example.
  • Pay 10 euros for cleaning when returning the car.
  • Pay an excess of 190 euros in the event of an accident. If the other party is at fault, we refund the amount paid.

02

Car rental

Rental terms

  • The minimum rental period is 24 hours.
  • The renter must present two identity documents (driving licence and passport).
  • The rental fee is paid for the whole period when the contract is signed, or, when paying by invoice, at least 2 days before the rental period begins.
  • A booking requires a deposit of 20% of the full rental price; the remainder is paid when the contract is signed.
  • The rental price includes VAT, motor third-party liability insurance and comprehensive (kasko) insurance. A security deposit of 200 EUR is charged separately and returned at the end of the rental.
  • The car is handed over and returned clean and with a full tank. If the tank is not full, the renter pays 2 EUR for every missing litre and from 10 EUR for washing.
  • The car is handed over and returned only at KERESE 25, Narva.
  • The renter is responsible for the completeness and sound condition of the rented car.
  • The renter must use only the fuel the car is designed for.
  • The lessor may refuse to rent out a car without giving a reason.
  • The daily mileage limit is 400 km.
